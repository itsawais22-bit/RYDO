Our Mission

RYDO was founded to restore balance between riders and drivers. Traditional corporate ride apps take up to 30% of drivers' daily earnings and subject passengers to unpredictable, inflated surge pricing during rain or peak hours.

With RYDO, riders propose what they are willing to pay, drivers propose transparent counter-offers, and a deal is made without any middleman manipulation.

5% Platform Fee

Drivers take home 95% of every single fare. No hidden vehicle deductions.

Safety First

Strict biometric, driving license, and CNIC verification for every driver.

Equal Respect
